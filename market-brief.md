# Cook & Bake — Market Brief

## Positioning

Small-group, hands-on cooking and baking courses in Singapore that teach repeatable techniques and produce practical, take-home outcomes.

## Three personas

1. **Experience-led professional** — wants a memorable evening, date or gift. Chooses by location, visual result, timetable and an inclusive experience. Likely courses: Cookies, Cupcakes & Cake Pops, Thai Street Food and Knife Skills.
2. **Serious home-skill builder** — wants live feedback and techniques that transfer to a home kitchen. Chooses by hands-on time, progression and class size. Likely courses: Sourdough, Bread Fundamentals, Italian and French Foundations.
3. **Outcome-driven specialist** — wants a portfolio piece, side-business capability or specialist skill. Chooses by instructor authority, advanced output, ingredients and credentials. Likely courses: Viennoiserie, Wedding Cake, Chocolate and Sushi.

## Five courses to launch first

- CUL-210 Knife Skills — S$160, one three-hour session.
- BAK-108 Cookie & Biscuit Baking — S$180, one three-hour session.
- CUL-202 Thai Street Food — S$540, three three-hour sessions.
- BAK-101 Artisan Sourdough — S$680, four three-hour sessions.
- CUL-208 Vegetarian & Vegan Cuisine — S$540, three three-hour sessions.

## Pricing notes

Sampled commercial workshops cost S$118–S$220 per person. Academy single sessions cost S$160–S$220. Multi-week courses cost S$160–S$245 per assumed weekly lesson and generally exceed ABC package pricing. SkillsFuture-supported alternatives create a lower price anchor.
