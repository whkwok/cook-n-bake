# Policies — Cook & Bake Academy Singapore

## Refunds and cancellations

Cancel 7 or more days before the first session for a full refund. Cancel 3–6 days before for a 50% refund or a free transfer to another intake. Cancellations within 2 days of the first session, and no-shows, are not refunded. Transfers are allowed once per enrolment.

## If we cancel a class

If a course does not reach 4 learners, or we must cancel, you get a full refund or a free transfer — your choice.

## Discounts

Early-bird: 10% off when you pay 30 or more days before the start date. Bring-a-friend: 5% off each when two learners sign up together. Discounts cannot be combined, and there are no other discounts.

## Food safety and hygiene

All instructors hold a valid food-hygiene certification. Learners wash hands on arrival, tie back hair and do not work while unwell. Raw fish in CUL-203 is sushi-grade and handled on dedicated boards.

## Privacy

We use your details only to run your course and, if you opt in, to send our monthly newsletter. You can withdraw consent at any time by emailing us. We never sell your data.

## Photography

We may photograph classes for social media. Tell your instructor if you prefer not to appear.
