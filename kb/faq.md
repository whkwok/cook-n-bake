# Frequently asked questions — Cook & Bake Academy Singapore

## Who are your courses for?

Home cooks, hobby bakers, aspiring food entrepreneurs and career changers. Beginner courses need no experience.

## How big are the classes?

A maximum of 12 learners per class, each at their own bench or station.

## Is there a minimum age?

Learners must be 16 years old or older. One-session workshops (BAK-106, BAK-108) accept children aged 12–15 years old with a paying guardian.

## Are ingredients included?

Yes. The course fee covers all ingredients, equipment and aprons. You take home what you make.

## How do I pay?

By PayNow, credit card or bank transfer. Your seat is confirmed once payment is received.

## Do you offer certificates?

Yes — a Certificate of Completion for learners who attend at least 75% of sessions. Our courses are not SkillsFuture Credit claimable.

## Do you do corporate team building?

Yes, for groups of 8 to 24 at either campus. Email us with your preferred date and group size for a quote.

## Do you sell gift vouchers?

Yes, in S$50 steps, valid for 12 months on any course.

## What if I miss a session?

You may join the same session in another running intake within 3 months, subject to space. Missed sessions are not refunded.

## Can I cater for my allergy?

Tell us when you sign up. We can often substitute an ingredient, but our kitchens handle nuts, gluten, dairy, eggs and shellfish, so we cannot guarantee an allergen-free environment. BAK-104 Macaron Masterclass is not suitable for nut allergies.

## What should I wear?

Closed-toe shoes and comfortable clothes. Tie back long hair. Aprons are provided.

## Is there parking?

Orchard Road Bakehouse has paid parking in the building. Bukit Timah Culinary Campus has free parking after 7pm.
