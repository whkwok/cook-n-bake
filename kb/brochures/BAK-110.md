# Korean & Asian Bakery (BAK-110)

**Cook & Bake Academy Singapore** · Bakery · Intermediate

## Overview

Tangzhong milk bread, salt bread, cream cheese garlic bread and Hong Kong egg tarts.

## What you will learn

- Make tangzhong and water-roux doughs
- Shape salt bread and filled buns
- Bake Hong Kong-style egg tarts
- Make a cream cheese garlic filling
