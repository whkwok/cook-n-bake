# Artisan Sourdough Bread Baking (BAK-101)

**Cook & Bake Academy Singapore** · Bakery · Beginner

## Overview

Build and keep a starter, then bake open-crumb country loaves, baguettes and a seeded rye at home.

## What you will learn

- Feed and maintain a wild-yeast starter
- Mix, stretch-and-fold and shape by hand
- Read proof by feel and by the poke test
- Score and bake in a home oven with a Dutch oven
