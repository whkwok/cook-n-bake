# French Culinary Foundations (CUL-204)

**Cook & Bake Academy Singapore** · Cooking · Beginner

## Overview

The classical base: knife cuts, stocks, the mother sauces, roasting, poaching and plating.

## What you will learn

- Classic cuts: julienne, brunoise, chiffonade
- Make brown and white stocks
- Cook the five mother sauces
- Roast, poach and plate a French menu
