# Thai Street Food Cooking (CUL-202)

**Cook & Bake Academy Singapore** · Cooking · Beginner

## Overview

Pad thai, green curry from a fresh paste, som tam and mango sticky rice.

## What you will learn

- Pound a green curry paste by hand
- Balance sweet, sour, salty and hot
- Wok-fry pad thai for two
- Steam sticky rice
