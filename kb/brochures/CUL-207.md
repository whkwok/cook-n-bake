# Healthy Meal Prep & Nutrition (CUL-207)

**Cook & Bake Academy Singapore** · Cooking · Beginner

## Overview

Plan, batch-cook and store a balanced week of lunches and dinners.

## What you will learn

- Plan a balanced weekly menu
- Batch-cook grains, proteins and vegetables
- Store and reheat food safely
- Read nutrition labels

## Schedule, fee and class size

- Duration: 2 weeks, one 3-hour hands-on session per week
- When: every Sunday 10:00am–1:00pm
- Next intakes: 2026-10-11 and 2026-12-06
- Course fee: S$360 (all ingredients and equipment included)
- Class size: maximum 12 learners
- Level: Beginner

## Allergens and what to bring

- Ingredients used: varies by menu; vegetarian options available.
- Bring: Five meal-prep containers.
