# Macaron Masterclass (BAK-104)

**Cook & Bake Academy Singapore** · Bakery · Intermediate

## Overview

French and Italian meringue macarons with ganache and buttercream fillings.

## What you will learn

- Age egg whites and sift almond flour correctly
- Macaronage to the right lava-like flow
- Pipe even shells and control feet
- Fill, mature and store macarons
