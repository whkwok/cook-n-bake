# Indian Curry & Spices (CUL-206)

**Cook & Bake Academy Singapore** · Cooking · Beginner

## Overview

Whole and ground spices, tempering, North and South Indian curries, dal and flatbreads.

## What you will learn

- Toast, grind and temper spices
- Build a masala base
- Cook a North and a South Indian curry
- Make dal and chapati
