# Knife Skills & Kitchen Essentials (CUL-210)

**Cook & Bake Academy Singapore** · Cooking · Beginner

## Overview

A one-session workshop: grip, sharpening and the core cuts every home cook needs.

## What you will learn

- Hold and control a chef's knife safely
- Sharpen on a whetstone and hone
- Dice onions, mince garlic and chiffonade herbs
