# Chocolate & Confectionery Making (BAK-105)

**Cook & Bake Academy Singapore** · Bakery · Intermediate

## Overview

Tempering, moulded bonbons, pralines, caramels and hand-rolled truffles.

## What you will learn

- Temper dark, milk and white chocolate by seeding
- Cast shells and fill moulded bonbons
- Cook caramel and nougat to temperature
- Hand-roll and enrobe truffles
