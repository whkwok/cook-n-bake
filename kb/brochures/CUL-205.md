# Chinese Wok Cooking (CUL-205)

**Cook & Bake Academy Singapore** · Cooking · Beginner

## Overview

Wok hei, velveting, stir-fries, fried rice and Cantonese sauces.

## What you will learn

- Season and control a carbon-steel wok
- Velvet chicken and beef
- Cook egg fried rice with wok hei
- Make oyster, black bean and sweet-sour sauces
