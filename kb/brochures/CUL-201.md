# Italian Cuisine Mastery (CUL-201)

**Cook & Bake Academy Singapore** · Cooking · Intermediate

## Overview

Fresh pasta, risotto, braises and regional Italian classics cooked from scratch.

## What you will learn

- Roll fresh egg pasta and filled shapes
- Cook risotto to all'onda
- Braise osso buco and make ragù
- Plate a four-course Italian menu
