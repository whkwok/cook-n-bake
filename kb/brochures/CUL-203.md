# Japanese Sushi & Sashimi (CUL-203)

**Cook & Bake Academy Singapore** · Cooking · Intermediate

## Overview

Sushi rice, knife work for sashimi, maki, nigiri and temaki with safe raw-fish handling.

## What you will learn

- Season and cool sushi rice
- Break down and slice fish for sashimi
- Roll hosomaki and uramaki
- Form nigiri and handle raw fish safely
