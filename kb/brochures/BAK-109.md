# Pie & Tart Specialist (BAK-109)

**Cook & Bake Academy Singapore** · Bakery · Intermediate

## Overview

Flaky and sweet shortcrust, blind baking, custards, lemon tart and a lattice fruit pie.

## What you will learn

- Make flaky and pâte sucrée pastry
- Line and blind-bake without shrinkage
- Cook crème pâtissière and lemon curd
- Weave a lattice top
