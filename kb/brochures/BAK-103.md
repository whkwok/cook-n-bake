# Wedding Cake Design & Decoration (BAK-103)

**Cook & Bake Academy Singapore** · Bakery · Advanced

## Overview

Stacked, dowelled and decorated tiered cakes, including fondant covering and sugar flowers.

## What you will learn

- Level, fill and crumb-coat stable layers
- Dowel and stack a three-tier cake safely
- Cover with fondant with sharp edges
- Model gumpaste roses and piped lace
