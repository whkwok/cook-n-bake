# Grilling & BBQ Mastery (CUL-209)

**Cook & Bake Academy Singapore** · Cooking · Intermediate

## Overview

Charcoal control, direct and indirect heat, marinades, rubs and satay.

## What you will learn

- Set up two-zone charcoal heat
- Grill to temperature with a probe
- Make rubs, marinades and satay sauce
- Rest and carve meat
