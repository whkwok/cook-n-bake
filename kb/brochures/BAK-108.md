# Cookie & Biscuit Baking (BAK-108)

**Cook & Bake Academy Singapore** · Bakery · Beginner

## Overview

A one-session workshop: chewy, crisp and iced cookies, shortbread and biscotti.

## What you will learn

- Control spread with butter temperature
- Make shortbread and twice-baked biscotti
- Flood and pipe royal icing

- Bring: Nothing — boxes are provided. Suitable for ages 12+ with a guardian.
