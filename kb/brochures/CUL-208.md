# Vegetarian & Vegan Cuisine (CUL-208)

**Cook & Bake Academy Singapore** · Cooking · Beginner

## Overview

Satisfying plant-based mains, tofu and tempeh, grain bowls and dairy-free desserts. Fully vegan menu.

## What you will learn

- Press, marinate and crisp tofu and tempeh
- Build umami without meat
- Make plant-based sauces and dressings
- Cook a dairy-free dessert

## Allergens and what to bring

- Ingredients used: soy, may contain nuts and sesame; no meat, dairy or eggs.
- Bring: Containers for tasting portions.
- Tell us about allergies when you sign up. Our kitchens handle nuts, gluten, dairy, eggs and shellfish, so we cannot guarantee an allergen-free environment.
