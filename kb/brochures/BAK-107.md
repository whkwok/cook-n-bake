# Bread Making Fundamentals (BAK-107)

**Cook & Bake Academy Singapore** · Bakery · Beginner

## Overview

Yeasted breads for the home baker: sandwich loaf, focaccia, soft milk buns and pizza dough.

## What you will learn

- Measure by weight and understand hydration
- Knead by hand and check the windowpane
- Shape a tin loaf, rolls and focaccia
- Bake with steam in a home oven
