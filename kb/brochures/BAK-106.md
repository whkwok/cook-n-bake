# Cupcake & Cake Pops Workshop (BAK-106)

**Cook & Bake Academy Singapore** · Bakery · Beginner

## Overview

A one-session workshop: bake, frost and decorate cupcakes and cake pops.

## What you will learn

- Bake a light vanilla and chocolate sponge
- Pipe buttercream swirls and rosettes
- Roll, dip and decorate cake pops

## Schedule, fee and class size

- Duration: 1 week, one 3-hour hands-on session per week
- When: every Saturday 2:00pm–5:00pm
- Next intakes: 2026-10-10 and 2026-12-05
- Course fee: S$220 (all ingredients and equipment included)
- Class size: maximum 12 learners
- Level: Beginner

## Where

Orchard Road Bakehouse, 88 Orchard Road, #03-08, Singapore 238839 (fictitious). 5 minutes from Somerset MRT. Paid parking in the building.

## Allergens and what to bring

- Ingredients used: wheat (gluten), dairy, eggs.
- Bring: Nothing — boxes are provided. Suitable for ages 12+ with a guardian.
- Tell us about allergies when you sign up. Our kitchens handle nuts, gluten, dairy, eggs and shellfish, so we cannot guarantee an allergen-free environment.
