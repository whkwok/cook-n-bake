# French Pastry & Viennoiserie (BAK-102)

**Cook & Bake Academy Singapore** · Bakery · Intermediate

## Overview

Laminated dough from scratch: croissants, pain au chocolat, kouign-amann and Danish pastries.

## What you will learn

- Make and lock in a butter block
- Laminate with three single folds
- Proof and bake croissants with an open honeycomb
- Shape pain au chocolat, kouign-amann and Danish
